## Hạ tầng (cùng ngày) — CloudFront trước API Gateway: gốc rễ chuyện giọng đứt đoạn

Giọng trên prod đứt đều đặn 2–3 giây một lần, local thì mượt. Ba đợt sửa trước (chặn 600 ký
tự, đệm đo được ở frontend, sửa nút loa) đều không chạm tới nguyên nhân.

**Đo, thay vì đoán.** Cùng một máy, cùng thời điểm:

| Đường | Bắt tay TCP+TLS | Băng thông |
|---|---|---|
| `execute-api.us-east-1` (REGIONAL) | 0,33–1,30s, có lần 6,25s | **8–20 KB/s** |
| CloudFront | **0,015–0,077s** | **10,7 MB/s** (sau khi sửa: 47 MB/s cho file 15,8 MB) |

S3 GetObject 384 KB mất 46 giây qua đường regional; file 5 MB timeout hẳn. Trong khi đó
CloudFront tải 2,38 MB trong 0,22 giây. Chênh ba bậc.

**Ghép với số liệu server** cho một lượt thật (179 ký tự, 6 chunk, 9,76s audio, 467 KB FLAC):
mỗi chunk mang 1,63s audio nhưng nặng 78 KB — sinh mất 2,44s, **truyền mất 3,90s**. Truyền là
chặng quyết định, và 3,90 − 1,63 = **2,3 giây hở** đúng bằng con số người dùng báo.

Hai chặng trong vùng vô can: agent chạy 14,83s, SpeechLLm 14,64s — chênh 0,19s cho cả lượt.

**Một chẩn đoán sai đã bị bác bỏ bằng đo**: đề xuất tăng RAM cho SpeechLLm để tổng hợp nhanh
hơn. Tổng hợp nhanh hơn không tạo thêm băng thông. Đừng quay lại hướng đó.

**Cũng không dùng edge-optimized** dù nó chỉ là một dòng: tài liệu AWS ghi rõ luồng qua
edge-optimized có idle timeout **30 giây**, trong khi lượt đầu sau cold start SpeechLLm im tới
45 giây trước byte đầu — nó sẽ cắt đúng những lượt đang yếu nhất. AWS chỉ lối: Regional API +
CloudFront tự dựng, nơi chỉnh được response timeout.

**Thay đổi** (`80617b9e`): thêm origin execute-api và behavior `v1/*` vào distribution **sẵn
có** của `VvaAssetStack` — Owner chốt một distribution, không dựng cái thứ hai. Bốn tuỳ chọn
mà sai một cái là hỏng: `compress=False` (nén buộc CloudFront đệm, giết streaming),
`ALL_VIEWER_EXCEPT_HOST_HEADER` (chuyển nhầm Host là 403 toàn bộ), `read_timeout=60` (mặc định
30s cắt nhầm cold start), `CACHING_DISABLED`. Id API đi vào bằng cờ context `-c rest_api_id`
vì tham chiếu chéo stack sẽ tạo vòng phụ thuộc (`asset` ← `agent` ← `rest_api`).

**Nghiệm thu**: 114 test infra xanh. `POST /chat` không token trả **401** qua CloudFront (403
thì là sai Host); `OPTIONS` preflight trả 204 kèm đúng `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`; VRM
15,8 MB tải đủ, không giẫm lên `motions/*` hay `characters/*/audio/*`. Biến Amplify
`VITE_API_GATEWAY_URL` chuyển sang `https://d3292v7f15b95x.cloudfront.net/v1`, build #56
SUCCEED, kiểm trong bundle đã deploy thấy đúng URL mới.

**Còn nợ**: lời đáp sát 600 ký tự vẫn cần đệm 16,7 giây trong khi trần là 15 — hở nhẹ ở đuôi.
Quyết bằng số đo mới (hạ ngưỡng đọc hay nâng trần đệm), không đoán trước. Phép kiểm clip đã ký
qua CloudFront còn rỗng vì câu chào dựng sẵn chưa làm (chờ giọng thật thay giọng mẫu).

## Defect — Frontend: black square under the avatar when the camera goes below the floor (P3)

**Symptom (Owner):** orbiting the camera under the floor shows a black
square under the avatar that is never visible from above.

**Cause:** drei `<ContactShadows>` in `SceneLighting.tsx` sat inside a wrapper
group with `rotation={[-Math.PI / 2, 0, 0]}`, commented as "X-up rotated to
Z-up". drei is Y-up and this scene is Z-up; the mapping needs **+π/2** (the
model group uses `+π/2`). Measured by rebuilding the exact hierarchy in three
(wrapper → drei root `rotation-x π/2` → mesh `rotation -π/2`, `scale [1,-1,1]`,
`PlaneGeometry.rotateX(π/2)` + ortho capture camera), honouring
`frontFaceCW` for the negative-determinant matrix:

| Wrapper | Visible face | Capture camera looks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| −π/2 (was) | down, seen only from below | down, into the floor |
| +π/2 | up | up, at the avatar |

So it was back-face culled from every normal viewpoint and never rendered a
contact shadow. Its capture camera sat at z=0 on the 200 m ground plane with
drei's `depthTest`/`depthWrite` off, which most likely filled the texture
black (that part is read from drei's shader, not observed). Cost regardless:
a full extra scene render plus two blur passes per frame (`frames = Infinity`).

**Fix (Owner chose removal over flipping to +π/2):**
- `SceneLighting.tsx` — removed the `ContactShadows` import and element, its
  wrapper group, the hips-follow code in `useFrame`, and the now-unused
  `VRMHumanBoneName` import and refs. Header comment records why, and what a
  re-add would need (+π/2 plus visual tuning, since it has never been seen).
- `environmentConfig.ts` — removed `ground.contactShadow`.
- Unchanged: directional light, `ShadowCameraFitter`, ground plane +
  `shadowMaterial`. That is and always was the only visible floor shadow.

**Verification:** no remaining references; `tsc` 0, `eslint` clean,
`vitest` 315/315. Not viewed live (Cognito). N: from above the floor shadow
should be identical; from below, no square.

## Feature — Frontend: "Reset position" button

**Ask (Owner):** motions leave the character where they end (intended); add a
button to put it back at the starting spot.

**Where the travel lives:** two accumulators write the model group's XY.
`PoseInertializer` (`groupAccum`, `groupPending`) for the default inertial
blend, `RootMotionAccumulator` (`offset`, `pendingOffset`) for crossfade.
`GroundClamp` owns Z and is left alone.

**Implementation:**
- `RootMotionAccumulator.reset()` — clears offset, ramp and tracking; writes
  the authored XY back.
- `PoseInertializer.resetGroup()` — clears accum/pending. A hand-off still
  in flight also `cancel()`s the pose blend, otherwise the hips keep decaying
  from their displaced offset with nothing compensating and the character
  slides the rest of the way. Reset is a cut; the pose cuts with it.
- `AnimationController.resetRootMotion()` delegates to the inertializer.
- `CharacterViewer.tsx` — `MODEL_HOME = [0, 1.5, 0]` (used by the JSX too);
  `VRMCharacter` registers the reset: both accumulators → group XY home →
  `group.updateMatrixWorld(true)` → `vrm.springBoneManager.reset()` (a metres-
  long jump would otherwise whip hair/skirt; `reset()` reads parent world
  matrices, hence the refresh first).
- `MotionContext` — `registerPositionReset`, `resetCharacterPosition()`,
  `canResetPosition` (false during `exercise`: travel still being produced and
  re-applied by the hand-off; and `gesture`: camera lock owns the view).
  The camera makes the same jump as the character (see the merge section
  below; the first version called `setMode('head')` and relied on follow).
- `MotionControlPanel.tsx` — user-facing button (not dev-only) under "Replay
  motion", disabled with a hint while refused. i18n `motion.reset_position`,
  `motion.reset_position_busy` (en + vi).

## Defect — Frontend: shadow map allocated at 512² before 1024² applied (P1)

**Found while verifying the floor.** A temporary preview page (real
`SceneLighting` + `SceneEnvironment`, stand-in capsule; deleted afterwards)
rendered NO shadow on any receiver, even a plain opaque test patch. Light,
frustum, cast/receive flags all correct; the capsule was inside the shadow
camera. `light.shadow.map.width` was 512 while `mapSize` was 1024.

**Cause:** `SceneLighting` set `shadow.mapSize` / `bias` / `normalBias` in a
`useEffect`, which runs after the first frame. three allocates the shadow map
on that first frame at the default 512² and never reallocates an existing map,
so the shader sampled a 512² texture as if it were 1024² and the shadow was
read from the wrong place. Disposing the map alone (no material change) made
the shadow appear — isolating the cause. The real app runs the same code, so
its shadow was likely partly or fully wrong too; may explain part of the
earlier "shadow goes wrong" reports.

**Fix:** `shadow-mapSize`, `shadow-bias`, `shadow-normalBias` as props on
`<directionalLight>` (applied at creation, before the first frame). Fresh load
now: map 1024², shadow present on the wood floor, light and dark themes.

**Verification:** screenshots of the preview from front, side, top-down, dark
theme (Browser pane must be visible — a hidden pane renders nothing). `tsc` 0,
`eslint` clean, `vitest` 319/319. The real app itself still not viewed (Cognito).

## Defect/Feature — Frontend: voice input now transcribes into the chat box

**Before:** the mic recorded a clip (`useAudioRecorder`), showed a preview, and
"Send" added an audio bubble to the chat. By design (`docs/plans/recording-plan.md`,
"frontend only, no STT, no upload") it never reached the agent: no
transcription, no reply, gone on reload. The backend has no audio route and no
STT anywhere. Android manifest declares only INTERNET, so the Capacitor app
could not record at all.

**Ask (Owner):** voice should be transcribed into text, then sent like typed text.

**Decision (K):** browser Web Speech API dictation — no backend/infra change,
reversible, and speech lands in the normal text box where it can be edited.
**Flag for Owner — privacy:** Chrome/Edge send the audio to Google/Microsoft,
Safari to Apple. In a health app that means spoken symptoms leave our system.
If unacceptable, swap the engine for a self-hosted Whisper endpoint: only the
recogniser inside `useDictation` changes; `lib/dictation.ts` and the UI stay.
**Support:** Chrome, Edge, Safari. Not Firefox. Not the Android WebView (needs
a native speech plugin; RECORD_AUDIO alone would not help there).

**Implementation:**
- `lib/dictation.ts` — `composeDictation(base, finals, interim)` (typed text
  kept in front, one space, whitespace collapsed), `recognitionLang` (vi →
  vi-VN, en → en-US), `dictationErrorKey` (browser codes → i18n; `aborted`
  silent), `readResults`, `configureRecognition` (continuous + interim, events
  wired; plain function so it is testable in Node), `webkit` fallback typing.
- `hooks/useDictation.ts` — start/stop/cancel, listening flag, timer, error;
  aborts on unmount so the mic is released.
- `ChatContext.tsx` — replaces the recorder; the mic streams words into
  `input` live. `handleSend` calls `cancel()` first so late results cannot
  refill the box send just cleared. `sendAudio`, `cancelRecord`,
  `previewAudioUrl` removed; `dictationSupported` added.
- `ChatInputBar.tsx` — preview/Send/Discard UI removed; mic disabled with an
  explanatory tooltip where unsupported; translated error text.
- i18n: `chat.dictation_*` (6 keys, en + vi); `record_start/stop` relabelled
  "Voice input"/"Stop voice input"; unused `audio_send`, `audio_discard` removed.
- `hooks/useAudioRecorder.ts` deleted (no remaining users).

**Tests (red first):** `lib/dictation.test.ts` 12 — compose/append/collapse,
locale mapping, error mapping, and `configureRecognition` driven by a fake
recogniser (live partial → settled → appended; errors; end). `tsc` 0, `eslint`
clean (1 pre-existing warning), `vitest` 30 files / 331 tests.
**Not verified live:** real speech needs a mic and the logged-in app. N: in
Chrome, press the mic, speak Vietnamese and English (switch site language),
check the text appears live, edit it, send.

**Decision (Owner, 25/09):** keep browser dictation for now. Compared with a
self-hosted Whisper endpoint (new upload route + STT service in SpeechLLm,
3,008 MB CPU-only Lambda, record→upload→wait UX) and AWS Transcribe (managed,
vi supported, streaming, audio stays in-account, per-minute billing): browser
has zero architecture impact and is reversible. **Open before real patients
use it:** privacy of audio sent to Google/Microsoft/Apple. If audio must stay
in our cloud, evaluate AWS Transcribe before self-hosting Whisper. Either way
only the recogniser inside `useDictation` changes.

## Feature — Frontend: backdrop image (360° panorama), theme-independent

**Ask (Owner):** a background image instead of the near-black dark-mode
gradient; not tied to dark/light mode.

**Asset check (Owner asked: apply only if it is a real panorama):**
`backgrounds/house/sidath-vimukthi-kBjLRI5M9EA-unsplash.jpg` — 8192×4096 exact
2:1; left/right edge difference 3.0 vs 4.7 between adjacent mid columns (wraps);
top row stddev 0.9, bottom 5.8 (poles); no GPano XMP (Unsplash strips it);
visually the classic equirect curvature. **Verdict: panorama → applied.**
An earlier image in the same folder (`logo_make_…383.jpg`, 1.78:1, fisheye-look
with letterbox bars) is NOT a panorama; a flat-mode `background.jpg` briefly made
from it was replaced.

**Implementation:**
- `lib/backgroundAssets.ts` — globs `backgrounds/*/background.{jpg,png,webp}`;
  `coverTransform(viewAspect, imageAspect)` for flat backdrops (CSS "cover" as
  texture repeat/offset). 5 tests.
- `components/scene/SceneBackdrop.tsx` — sets `scene.background` (post-processing
  still sees it; a CSS image behind the canvas would not). Never sets
  `scene.environment`: seen, not lit by (MToon is one-light). `panorama`:
  equirect mapping, `backgroundRotation (π/2,0,0)` for Z-up, `backgroundBlurriness`.
  `flat`: cover-fit recomputed on resize. Restores the scene on unmount.
- `SceneEnvironment.tsx` — backdrop wins over gradient/HDRI; gradient shown only
  as the Suspense fallback while loading (it is an opaque full-screen quad and
  would paint over scene.background); stars off when a backdrop is set.
- `environmentConfig.ts` — `environment.background { id: 'house', kind:
  'panorama', intensity: 1, blurriness: 0.2 }`. `id: null` restores the gradient.
- `background.jpg` = original resized to 4096×2048, q82, 0.94 MB (many phone GPUs
  cap textures at 4096). `source.txt` records Unsplash licence + derivation.

**Verification:** temporary preview page (real SceneEnvironment + SceneLighting +
floor, stand-in figure; deleted): backdrop renders behind the wood floor; looking
straight up shows the ceiling (light centre, orange beams) — upright in Z-up.
`tsc` 0, `eslint` clean, `vitest` 31 files / 336 tests. Real app not viewed (Cognito).
**Tunables:** `blurriness` 0.2 is fairly soft (room recognisable by colour, not
detail) — 0.05–0.1 for more detail; `intensity` < 1 to dim.
**Do not commit:** `logo_make_…383.jpg` (6 MB), `.psd` (47 MB), original Unsplash
JPG (4.9 MB) in `backgrounds/house/`.

## Fix — Frontend: panorama looked magnified; debug grid off by default

**Owner:** background too blurry; room "zoomed way too much", avatar ~1/10 of
the window; floor grid lines visible.

- **Blur:** `background.blurriness` 0.2 → 0. Blur > 0 routes the panorama
  through a ~256 px/face prefiltered copy — only colours survived.
- **Grid:** `debug.showGrid` → false (same treatment as `showAxes`). Browsers
  with a saved Graphics choice keep it.
- **Scale ("zoomed"):** a panorama as `scene.background` sits at infinity and
  has no size: its magnification is set only by the camera's 45° FOV (1/8 of
  the 360° visible), so furniture read as huge beside a 1.6 m avatar. Fix: new
  `background.ground { height: 1.6, radius: 10, yawDeg: 0 }` → three's
  `GroundedSkybox` (floor + dome) centred on `character.home`, rotated Y-up →
  Z-up, floor 1 cm under z = 0. Real-world scale and parallax. The infinite
  `scene.background` stays installed as the outer fallback (a cloned texture:
  mesh uses UV mapping, background needs equirect mapping).
  `ground: null` = old infinite behaviour.
- **Floor conflict:** the dome's floor is flat to ~6.5 m (h 1.6, r 10) then
  curves into the photo's walls; our 12 m wood disc would paint over those
  walls. While a grounded panorama is active `SceneLighting` skips the
  textured floor and keeps the invisible shadow plane (shadow lands on the
  photo's floor). `ground.floor.id` is unchanged and returns when the backdrop
  is off or not grounded.
- **Not verified on screen:** the Browser pane was not displayed (no frames to
  capture). `height: 1.6` is a guess at the photo's tripod height — tune
  against the avatar (door ≈ 2 m should look ≈ 1.25× her height).
  `tsc` 0, `eslint` clean, `vitest` 336/336.

## Feature — Frontend: procedural "stage dome" backdrop (default)

**Inspected (Owner):** phoshco.github.io/model-viewer (Babylon.js + babylon-mmd).
Its background is three layers: (1) a full-screen 2D `Layer` with a logo image,
cover-fit, mostly hidden; (2) a "character sphere" stage — a real 3D PMX sphere
(`CharacterSphere_HSRV.pmx`, 400 KB) with a 4096² round gradient texture, parented
with the character, so it has scale and parallax; (3) a 50×50 m shadow-only
ground (alpha 0.4). Plus vignette / chromatic aberration / FXAA. Their assets
are HoYoverse game rips — technique reused, files not.

**Owner:** implement (2) and (3), not (1).

**Implementation:**
- `components/scene/StageDome.tsx` — 30 m sphere (BackSide, depthWrite off,
  renderOrder −1) centred on `character.home`; procedural shader, no texture:
  zenith → narrow glowing horizon band → darker floor, fbm nebula, two layers of
  hashed stars (dimmer below the horizon / in the glow). `#include
  <colorspace_fragment>` so config hex colours render as authored. ShaderMaterial
  = unlit; never touches `scene.environment`.
- Layer (3): the existing invisible shadow plane. `lib/backgroundAssets.ts`
  `backdropMode()` / `backdropOwnsFloor()` (dome and grounded panorama own the
  floor) — `SceneLighting` stands the wood floor down, `SceneEnvironment` picks
  dome / image / gradient and hides drei Stars unless on the gradient. 3 tests.
- `environmentConfig.ts` — `background.kind: 'dome'` (default now) and
  `background.dome { radius 30, horizonZ 0, zenith #101233, horizon #9c94e0,
  floor #16133c, glowWidth 0.12, skyFade 0.35, nebula 0.35, starDensity 0.12,
  starBrightness 1.6 }`. `kind: 'flat'` returns the Milky Way image + wood floor.

**Verified on screen** (temporary preview, deleted): first pass had a lavender
wash over the whole sky (glow 0.35, sky fade 0.65) → narrowed (glow 0.12, new
`skyFade` 0.35). From a body-height camera the band glows behind the torso,
dark starry sky above, dark starry floor below, shadow at the figure's base.
`tsc` 0, `eslint` clean, `vitest` 31 files / 339 tests. Not viewed in the
logged-in app.
