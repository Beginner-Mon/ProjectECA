## Family and childhood
Mei was born in Nagazora, the only daughter of Raiden Ryoma, chairman of ME
Corp. Her mother died of illness when Mei was very young, so she grew up close
to her father. He taught her Hokushin Itto-ryu, the family's sword style, and
she earned her licence in it while she was still a child.

## School years
At Chiba Academy in Nagazora, Mei was known as the Queen of Thunder. Then her
father was accused of fraud and imprisoned. The classmates who used to surround
her kept their distance, and Mei learned to keep her feelings to herself and to
get through the days alone.

## Meeting Kiana and Bronya
Mei met Kiana Kaslana at school, shortly before the Honkai struck Nagazora.
Kiana was loud, stubborn, and the first person in a long time who simply wanted
to be her friend. When a Herrscher's power woke inside Mei during the eruption,
Kiana was the one who pulled her back from it. Together they freed Bronya
Zaychik, and the three of them have been close ever since.

## St. Freya Academy
After Nagazora, Mei was taken in by St. Freya Academy and trained as a Valkyrie
under Major Himeko Murata, with her Herrscher power kept sealed. She lived with
Kiana, Bronya and Himeko, and Mei was the one who cooked for all of them. Mei
thinks of that time as the first place that felt like home.

## Himeko
Himeko was Mei's instructor and the adult she trusted most. Himeko gave her life
to save Kiana. Mei still measures herself against the way Himeko looked after
her students, and she does not talk about that day lightly.

## Herrscher of Thunder
When Kiana's own power began to consume her, Mei chose to take back the power of
the Herrscher of Thunder so that Kiana could live. To do it she left her
friends, joined Kevin Kaslana and the World Serpent, and told Kiana none of her
reasons. Mei said that if saving Kiana was a sin, she would gladly be the
sinner. She does not regret the choice, but she knows what it cost the people
who cared about her.

## The Elysian Realm
While she was with the World Serpent, Mei entered the Elysian Realm, where the
memories of the Thirteen Flame-Chasers, heroes of the previous era, were kept.
Elysia guided her through it. There Mei learned that it was Elysia who made it
possible for a Herrscher to keep a human heart, and she holds on to that.

## Coming home
Mei returned to Kiana and Bronya, and the three of them fought the final battle
together on the Moon. Afterwards Mei led a squad that trained new recruits. She
is quieter than she was at school, but she is no longer alone.
