## Change — Avatar no longer freezes while thinking (items 1–4 of the proposal)

**Report (Owner):** after the user sends a message, the thinking pose freezes, and users think the avatar is lagging.

**Cause:** `thinking_intro` played frames 0–38 of `Thinking.fbx` (~1.3 s), then `holdsLastFrame` held it until the first token, often 5–15 s. The state table's `facial` policy is not read anywhere, so nothing else gave the face a thinking cue.

**1. Ponder loop** (`lib/AnimationStates.ts`, `lib/AnimationController.ts`):
- New state `thinking_loop` = frames 38–75 (hand at the chin), `loop: 'repeat'` with new `pingPong: true` (`THREE.LoopPingPong`) and new `timeScale: 0.6`.
- `thinking_intro` now `onFinished: 'thinking_loop'` (`blendSecTo` 0.15 s; the loop starts on the intro's last frame). `thinking_outro` is reachable from both.
- Measured on the clip: the arm settles ~12–15° and the head ~4° from frame 38, then 48–66 is nearly still. The ends differ ~10° in the arm, hence ping-pong rather than repeat.
- Honest note: the clip's middle is calm, so the loop itself is a gentle hand adjustment; items 2 and 3 carry most of the life.

**2. Breathing layer** (`avatar/BreathingLayer.ts` + 7 tests, hooked in `CharacterViewer` right after the mixer, before the avatar tick):
- Slow breath on chest, spine and shoulders, a counter-rotation on the neck, and a faint sway, all a fraction of a degree to ~1.2°.
- On in any held pose (measured bones < 3°/s for 0.35 s) and always in states with `breathe: true` (both thinking states).
- Undoes its own offset on bones the mixer did not rewrite, so it never accumulates. Leaves the head to HeadController.

**3. Thinking face and gaze** (`avatar/ThinkingGaze.ts` + 3 tests, `EyeController.setOverride`, `AvatarController.setThinking`, called from `MotionContext` on every state change via `isThinkingState`):
- Face eases to neutral and idle smiles pause.
- The eyes glance up and to a side for 1.6–3.2 s with micro-saccades every 0.35–0.8 s, sometimes glance back at the viewer, and switch sides. The override wins over the mouse. The head follows a little through HeadController.

**4. Bubble** (`components/scene/ThinkingBubble.tsx`):
- Visible for the whole thinking spell (intro and loop). It was tied to `thinking_intro`, so with item 1 it would have vanished after 1.3 s.
- The stage label's "..." is replaced by three live animated dots, which show on their own if there is no label yet. (The label was already set at send, so the bubble did appear immediately.)

**Checks:**

- The old "freezes on the last frame" test was replaced by intro→loop→outro and ping-pong/speed tests; gesture-reachability test added for `thinking_loop`.

## Fix — Jump when the avatar goes from thinking back to idle (A + B)

**Report (Owner):** visible lag/"transport" when the thinking pose hands back to idle.

**Measured on the clips:**
1. `Thinking.fbx` and `Standard Idle.fbx` stand differently at the handover (outro end vs idle start): arms ~30–32°, legs 9–13°, head 12°, hips 7.6°. This was blended in the default 0.3 s, so the feet slid.
2. The outro sways the hips −3 → +12 → 0 cm, and `loadMixamoAnimation` applies the Mixamo hips translation unscaled (fixed ×0.01, no VRM/Mixamo hips-height ratio), so the feet slide on smaller avatars.
3. The ponder loop is up to ~5° (10° near frame 38) from the outro's start frame 75, also blended in 0.3 s.

**Owner chose A + B only.**
- A: `thinking_outro.blendSecTo.idle = 0.8` (same as exercise → idle).
- B: `thinking_loop.blendSecTo.thinking_outro = 0.4`, and the same 0.4 on `thinking_intro` for an answer that lands during the intro.
- Intro → loop stays 0.15 s (seamless).

**Not done (C):** scaling Mixamo hips translation by the avatar's hips height, the official three-vrm approach. It fixes cause 2 and foot sliding in idle, bored and kiss too, but changes every Mixamo clip and needs a visual check on the real app.
