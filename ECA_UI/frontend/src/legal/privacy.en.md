# Privacy Policy

**Effective:** {EFFECTIVE_DATE}

Project ECA ("ECA", "we") is a student project by Ordinary Studio. This policy explains what we collect when you use ECA, why, who else receives it, and how you can have it deleted. Some of what you tell ECA is about your health, so we have tried to be specific.

## 1. What we collect

- **Account details.** Your email address and display name. If you sign in with Google, we also receive the name, email address and profile picture on your Google account.
- **Your conversations.** The messages you send, ECA's replies, and short summaries ECA writes of each finished conversation so that it can remember them later. These can include health information you choose to share, such as pain, injuries, symptoms or exercises.
- **Feedback** you give on a reply: a thumbs up or down, the reasons you pick, and any comment you type.
- **Settings**, such as language, character and graphics options.
- **Technical logs.** When you chat, our servers record events for troubleshooting: timings, errors, and the first part of your message (up to 120 characters).

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell your data.

## 2. Why we use it

- To answer you, show your conversation history, and let ECA recall earlier conversations when you refer back to them.
- To keep the service working and safe: finding errors, measuring response times, and checking that safety advice (such as when to see a doctor) is given.
- To improve ECA, using feedback and logs. We do not use your conversations to train AI models.

By using ECA and sharing health information in a conversation, you agree to it being processed for these purposes. You can withdraw that agreement at any time by deleting the conversation or your account (see section 6).

## 3. Who else receives it

To produce a reply, your message and the recent conversation are sent to an AI model provider:

- **DeepSeek** (servers in China) answers by default.
- **Google Gemini** (Google LLC, United States) answers when DeepSeek is unavailable.
- If you add **your own API key** in Settings, your messages go to the provider you chose instead, under that provider's terms. Your key is stored only in your browser and is sent with each message; we do not save it on our servers or write it to our logs.

Other services we rely on:

- **Amazon Web Services** (United States) hosts the app, the voice and motion servers, the sign-in system (Amazon Cognito) and our logs.
- **Neon** (United States) hosts the database that stores your account and conversations.
- **Voice input**: when you use the microphone, your browser's own speech service turns speech into text. Chrome and Edge send the audio to Google or Microsoft; Safari sends it to Apple. ECA does not receive the audio.
- **Ko-fi**: if you choose to donate, you leave ECA, and Ko-fi's privacy policy applies.

Spoken replies and 3D movements are generated on our own servers and do not go to third parties.

## 4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in the United States, and is processed in China when DeepSeek answers. By using ECA you agree to this transfer outside Vietnam.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Account, conversations, summaries and feedback:** until you delete them or your account.
- **Technical logs:** up to 2 years.
- Deleted data can remain in encrypted database backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Delete a conversation** from your chat history in the app. Its messages and summaries are deleted with it.
- **Access, correct or delete** your data, **delete your account**, or **withdraw consent** by emailing {CONTACT_EMAIL} from the address on your account. We reply within 30 days.

## 7. Security

Connections to ECA are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to the database is limited to our servers and the team members who operate them. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you.

## 8. Children

ECA is not intended for anyone under 16.

## 9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
