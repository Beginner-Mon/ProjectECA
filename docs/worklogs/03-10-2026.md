## Change — Max zoom-out limited to 8 m

**Request (Owner):** zooming out too far in the free/panning camera breaks some features; limit it.

**Change:** `CameraConfig.ts` `maxDistance` 20 → **8 m**.
- It is global rather than manual-only: a zoom-out from head/hips switches the camera to manual, so a manual-only limit would snap the camera in by up to 12 m at that moment.
- The automatic framings all stay under it: `hips` 4.35 m on desktop and 4.97 m on phones, `head` 1 m.
- In practice this only limits the user's own zoom-out.

**Not limited:** how far a pan can move the orbit point. With pan + 8 m the camera can still be up to "pan distance + 8 m" from the character. If features still break after a long pan, the next step is to clamp the orbit point to within a few metres of the character (or of the floor disc, radius 12 m).
