# Worklog 26/09/2026

Continues [[25-09-2026]] on `local-testing-improve` (not pushed).

## Change — Frontend: stage dome without the bright horizon stripe

**Owner (screenshot of the live app):** a large bright purple line across the
middle of the screen — not wanted.

**Cause:** the dome's horizon colour (`#9c94e0`, lavender) plus the glow band
added on top of it (hard-coded 0.2), copied from the reference's look.

**Fix:** new `dome.glowStrength` uniform (was hard-coded) set to **0**;
`dome.horizon` → `#221f52`, a muted purple close to zenith/floor, so sky and
floor blend with no stripe. The old colour is noted in the config comment for
anyone who wants the glow back.

**Verified on screen** (temporary preview, deleted): even dark night sky with
stars, only a faint soft lightening at the horizon. `tsc` 0, `eslint` clean,
`vitest` 339/339.
