1. **Rail and chat showed over "loading character".**
   - The rail is fixed at z-10000, above the viewer overlay (z-9999), and the dock shows through the overlay's blur.
   - `MotionContext` now exposes `isAvatarReady` (set by `setAvatarReady`).

2. **Rail and chat stayed white in dark mode on a phone.**
   - `App.css` deliberately forced "white mobile surfaces in either app theme", and the JSX had `bg-white`.
   - The white token override now applies under `:root:not(.dark)` only. `bg-white` became `bg-card` (rail, conversation card, composer). The forced `dark` scope on the dock wrapper is gone, so the motion chips follow the theme too.
   - The rail gets a border in dark mode.
   - The rule's media query now matches the phone layout (`MOBILE_LAYOUT_QUERY`) instead of `width < 768px`, which missed phones held sideways.
3. **Loading text sat flush left on a phone.** `LoadingOverlay`'s label gets `text-center px-6 max-w-full`, so wrapped lines centre.
